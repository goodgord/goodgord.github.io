About

A trivia night in a box. Well, a zip.

Just Great Trivia is a pub trivia night, written fresh every week by John.

Each pack is the whole night: four rounds of questions (round two is the music round), three Who Am I, a puzzle sheet of eight pictures and four questions on the week's news. John writes it as Word documents and mp3s; you download it as one file.

The Host app turns that pack into the night. Import the pack, pick tonight's teams, and it puts the right thing in front of you at the right time: what to read out, what to play, whose scores to type in, and who won the round and why.

  • Written fresh

    A new night every week, written by John. The questions, the songs, the clues, the pictures and the news.

  • Runs from your laptop

    Offline and keyboard-driven. Space for the next thing. No pub wifi required.

  • Picks up where it left off

    Laptop dies mid-round? Reopen the app and you're back on the same question, scores intact.

  • A season, not just a night

    Teams carry over from week to week. Nights won, rounds won and bragging rights, kept for you.

How a night runs

Same order, every week. New questions, every week.

Teams write their answers on paper, swap sheets and mark each other's. You read, they groan, the app keeps score.

    Fifteen questions and a tie-break, read out one at a time. Teams write their answers.

    Who Am I

    Clues, one at a time, until someone shouts it out. First team to get it takes the points.

    The puzzle sheet

    Eight pictures on a printed page, handed out now. Teams chip away at it between rounds, and it's marked with round three.

    Three clips of five songs. Then the whole lot again, so teams can fill the gaps.

    Round three, plus the puzzle

    Fifteen more and a tie-break. When the sheets swap, the puzzle answers come out too, pictures and all.

    Marked together
  7. 07

    The last Who Am I

    One more shout before the home straight.

    Shouted · 2 points
  8. 08

    Round four, plus current events

    The final fifteen, then four questions on this week's news, worth five points each. It all gets marked together.

    4 questions × 5 points
  9. 09

    The winners

    The night's winner, every round winner and the final standings. The season table updates itself.

    Season updated

After every round, the sheets swap and the answers come out. You type in each team's total; the app lines up the results lowest to highest and names the winner.

Tie-breaks

Every round has a number question as its tie-break. It only matters if the top score is tied: closest answer wins.

One more rule. A team that's already won a round tonight can't win another while a tied team hasn't. The app applies it and tells you why, in one line.
