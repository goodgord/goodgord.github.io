Download the app

The Host app.

It runs the whole night from your laptop: what to read out, what to play, whose scores to type in, and who won the round and why.

It's a prompt, not a script. You know how to run a room. The app just makes sure the right question is in front of you when you need it, in letters you can read at arm's length in a dim pub.

The app comes with a demo night. Run it at home, press every key, and see how a night flows before you spend a cent.

What it does on the night.

  • The whole night, in order

    Every question, clip, clue and score, in the order you'll say them. No hunting for the next thing.

  • Keys, not clicks

    Run it without touching the trackpad.

    Space next ← back A answer S scores D display

  • No wifi? No worries.

    It runs offline. The pack, the music and the pictures all live on your laptop.

  • Picks up where it left off

    Laptop flat mid-round? Reopen the app and you're on the same question, scores intact.

  • Winners, worked out

    Type in each team's total. It lines up the results, runs the tie-break with a hint range, and explains any close call in one line.

  • A season of standings

    Teams carry over between nights. Nights won, rounds won, nights played and average scores, all kept.

  • A screen for the room

    Plug in a projector or a TV and the room gets its own display: the question, the clues, the results. Never an answer before you've read it out.

  • Pack problems, caught early

    If a pack is missing something, the app says exactly what, in plain words, before the night starts.

Buy a pack

One week. One night. One zip.

A pack is a whole night from John, written fresh that week.

Four rounds, each with a tie-break: three of fifteen questions, and round two, the music round, of three clips with five songs apiece. Three Who Am I. A puzzle sheet of eight pictures. Four questions on the week's news.

John writes it as Word documents and mp3s, and it comes to you as one file, sealed to your copy of the app. Drop it into the app and it checks the lot before you start.

How your packs are protected

Sealed to your app. Yours alone.

Every pack is sealed to the copy of the app it was bought for.

Your host key tells checkout which copy that is. The pack you download opens in your app and nowhere else, so forwarding the email or copying the file gets nobody anywhere.

That's why packs can't be shared. It keeps the answers a surprise on the night, and the pack you paid for stays yours.
